Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 January 2026

AI Job Market is committed to protecting your privacy and complies with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect limited technical data, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and interactions
  • Referral sources

We do not collect:

  • CVs or job applications
  • Payment information
  • Sensitive personal data

2. How Data Is Collected

Data is collected via:

  • Google Analytics
  • Google Search Console
  • Cookies and similar technologies

3. Cookies

Cookies are used to analyse traffic and improve site performance. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

4. How We Use Data

We use collected data to:

  • Monitor and improve website performance
  • Understand user behaviour
  • Improve content relevance and usability

We do not sell or rent personal data.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers, including:

  • Google Analytics
  • Cloudflare (hosting, security, and performance)

These services may process limited data under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained only as long as necessary for operational and improvement purposes.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

8. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect data, including secure hosting via Cloudflare.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through the website.
