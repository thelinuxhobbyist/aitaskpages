About AI Job Market

AI Job Market (aijobmarket.co.uk) is a UK-based job aggregation platform designed to help people discover opportunities in artificial intelligence, data, automation, and emerging technologies.

Our goal is simple: to make AI-related jobs easier to find in one place.

Rather than searching across multiple job boards, AI Job Market brings together relevant job listings from trusted third-party sources and presents them in a single, searchable platform. This helps job seekers save time and stay up to date with the fast-moving AI job market.

What We Do

  • Aggregate AI and technology-focused job listings from third-party job boards (currently including Reed)
  • Organise and categorise roles to improve discovery
  • Plan to expand coverage to additional sources such as Indeed and LinkedIn
  • Provide a fast, secure, and accessible browsing experience

What We Don't Do

  • We do not post jobs directly
  • We do not act as an employer or recruitment agency
  • We do not guarantee the accuracy, availability, or legitimacy of job listings

All job listings link to the original source where applications are completed.

Our Vision

As artificial intelligence continues to reshape industries, AI Job Market aims to become a trusted discovery platform for professionals exploring careers in AI and related technologies.
